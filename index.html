AGSL Visual Reference

    Interactive visuals for every AGSL built-in function.

    GT
    float, float2, float3, float4, half, half2, half3 or half4.
    BV
    bool2, bool3 or bool4.
    T
    float2, float3, float4, half2, half3, half4, int2, int3, int4, short2, short3 or short4.
    mat
    float2x2, float3x3, float4x4, half2x2, half3x3 or half4x4.

    Source of truth: the AGSL Quick Reference. Math runs with y pointing up on these canvases; in a real AGSL shader fragCoord.y points down. Color functions assume an sRGB working color space.
